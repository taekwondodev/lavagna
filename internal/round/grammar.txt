# Per-question round grammar

A call contains one or more questions. A level-1 title starts each question and
requires an id; `after` is optional and names question ids in the phase.

```markdown
# What should happen after a crash? {id="crash" after="storage"}
A concise lead for this question.

## Capire
Explain the current behavior and problem.

::: excerpt internal/store/store.go:40-46 45!1
`os.WriteFile` truncates before writing.
:::

## Confrontare
Compare alternatives and consequences.

## Decidere
- [atomic] Write a temporary file, then rename {recommended}
  => The old file remains intact until rename.
- [journal] Append records and recover the last valid record

A final paragraph may explain the recommendation.

# What should be retained? {id="retention"}
```

The second title above is a planned question: it has no body or artifact yet.
A later call can supply its complete question with the same id. A complete
question replaces its previous version together with its resources.

## Titles, ids and dependencies

- Question ids match `[a-z0-9][a-z0-9_-]{0,39}` and are unique in a phase.
- A title is one line, at most 120 characters.
- `after="id other-id"` names known questions; dependencies must not cycle.
- `now` is reserved for the present state of diagrams.

## Chapters and choices

- `## Capire`, `## Confrontare`, `## Decidere` are optional except Decidere for
  a complete question; use that order. `###` headings are content headings.
- A complete question has 2 to 6 options, each written `- [id] label`.
  Option ids use the question id pattern; `now` is reserved.
- At most one option ends in `{recommended}`.
- An indented `=> consequence` line gives that option's effect; at most one per
  option. A free-text answer is always available and needs no syntax.
- A bare title is planned and has no question artifact.
- `::: excerpt path:start-end 45!1` includes a source excerpt; `45!1` marks
  line 45 as a problem with badge 1. Separate marks with spaces.
- `{ref}`, `data-ref`, level-1 chapter headings, and shared/root resources are
  unsupported. Anchors are not part of per-question feedback.

## Resources and bounds

For `DIR/round.md`, question `crash` owns files only under `DIR/crash/`. A
question's frame is served at `/f/<key>/<question-id>/`; references name files
as `<question-id>/file`. Only that question's `.js` and `.css` run in its frame.
No resources are shared across questions.

Input text, resources and rendered excerpts are bounded to 4 MiB and at most
32 files per call. Retained question artifacts are bounded to 6 MiB. Resource
paths must be regular files below the question directory; symlinks are refused.

## Feedback

One Send returns `questions`, keyed by id, and optional `overview`. A question may
have `choice` or `answer` (never both), `messages`, and `images`; empty fields
are omitted. Above 2 KiB the outcome is deferred: choices remain visible,
messages and images become counts, and a free-text answer is `true`. Read full
feedback with `lavagna feedback SUBMISSION --question ID`, `--overview`, or
`--all`. The selectors are mutually exclusive. The default reprints the
summary. Unknown ids and questions without feedback in the selected batch are
errors.

A batch admits at most 32 KiB of text including answers, a 48 KiB record, and
8 screenshots of up to 10 MiB each. `close` and the one-day sweep remove
question artifacts and feedback.
